# Touch pilot — current operator decisions, 27 October 2026

The operator accepted the mission/observe-plan-act ideas from the supplied
10-page Claude PDF and rejected joystick controls. This update supersedes the
movement-stick proposals in the earlier work order. The PDF is design input;
its market/scientific claims have not been independently verified here.

First playable slice: **Bandenschalter**, then **Wächter und Energietor**. A short
window repeats without a global countdown or score penalty. Tap ground to move;
use explicit aim/prediction/fire modes for reflection. No keyboard/gamepad is
required. Shared arena/reward rules are in `shared/pilot.json`.

Stars are persistent, one-time rewards for goals, accurate prediction, successful
cover and hidden finds. Replays/retries/waiting do not mint duplicate rewards.
Unlocks at 100/160/230/300 total stars grant prism weapon, armor, speed and partial
camouflage. Stars are retained, not spent. All goals remain achievable with base
gear. Camouflage cannot remove every need for cover. Hidden gear/stars remain
optional and are found by exploration or a clue.

Each distinct completed mission earns two clue credits. Paid clues only reveal
optional secret landmarks/proximity, persist across retries, and do not repeatedly
charge for the same clue. Core controls, spoken mission explanation, safe retry
and the initial demonstration are always available without clue credits.

The operator expressly authorizes reuse of matching finished Kinderwelt audio.
Copy only semantically suitable public clips with original hashes/licence notices;
no family images, personal files, keys or broad asset import. Mission-specific
sentences require matching new recordings; do not splice old syllables or play a
math question as a mission description. Kinderwelt stays read-only.

The three-engine comparison is still requested. Godot and a native GDevelop WebGL pilot have been executed and debug APKs built;
Unity Editor/runtime activation is unavailable. See QUALITY.md for actual results. No
engine winner or unexecuted Android/device benchmark is claimed. Do not create
pull requests, merge or publish a release at this stage.
